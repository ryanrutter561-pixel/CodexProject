HOW IT WORKS: This is the story of the series, from Fredbear's Family Diner to the Mega Pizzaplex. Wherever fans disagree, you'll see a CONTESTED box. Pick a theory and the timeline rewrites itself, along with characters, connections and dates.

      

      

        
        
        
      

      

        

        

      

      

    


    
    

      

        
GRAND THEORIES: The big, complete readings of the series, each one a full timeline the fandom has rallied behind at some point. Pick one to read its condensed timeline and see what sets it apart, or load it into your canon. The % MATCH shows how close your current choices are to each one.

      

      

      

      

        
SIDE BY SIDE

        
Where the grand theories split. Gold marks a break from the mainstream Afton Saga, and ✓ marks an answer that matches your canon.

        

      

    


    
    

      

        
ARCADE: The series hides its darkest secrets in tiny 8-bit minigames. Pick a cabinet to watch a beat-by-beat replay (original pixel art, not the real game), then read what it reveals. Use NEXT, the arrow keys, or AUTO.

THEORY BOARD: Every open question in one place. Each option shows the case FOR and AGAINST, and the tag shows roughly how the community sees it, from CONFIRMED to FRINGE.

      

      

      

    


    
    

      
DOSSIERS: Select a character to open their file. Bios, aliases and forms all follow your chosen theories.

      

    


    
    

      
CONNECTIONS: Pick anyone to put them at the centre, and select a neighbour to follow the thread. family/ally harm depends on your theories
